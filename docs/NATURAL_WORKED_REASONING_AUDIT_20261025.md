# Audit natural worked reasoning before changing the training task

The owner proposed withholding naturally occurring intermediate work while
preserving its premises and ordered conclusion. This is worth investigating;
it is not established by the existing NTP gains or the closed future-span loss.
Finish and preserve the cheap-mixing comparison first. That comparison is now
complete:extra core execution helps at equal data,but not at matched work.

First perform a CPU-only feasibility audit,not another training campaign.
Use only previously accepted TRAIN mathematics documents from the existing
6.5B-source corpus extension. Restrict this initial convenience sample to raw
shards18–23. Select20,000 accepted documents by deterministic content-ID order.
Recover their exact original text using both URL and normalized-content hash;
verify the recorded TRAIN source-group identity. Open no DEV,TEST or fresh
reserve file. Do not publish source text,URLs,token arrays or answer strings.

Freeze two high-recall candidate classes before scanning:explicit problem/
solution markers with mathematical material;or mathematical material with an
explicit conclusion cue. These are candidates for inspection,not valid worked
examples. Rank each class by a fixed hash,sample at most100 per class and at
most5 per source group overall. Preserve low yield rather than broadening the
patterns after seeing outcomes. The audit has a3,600-second CPU cap,at most4
raw readers,and the existing512GiB memory/35GiB disk reserves. Record every
selected/recovered count,ambiguity and rejection;there are no optimizer steps.

Inspect the private sample for complete premises,derivable intermediate work,
a short original conclusion,absence of copied answers,diagram/reference
requirements,source diversity and fit within context. Agent inspection is not
an independent human or formal mathematical verification. Do not generate a
solution,target,paraphrase or missing premise. Any accepted view must later
retain original source spans and offset provenance;this scan creates no views.
A mask must remove work,not information needed to determine the answer.

[Coconut](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06769) motivates a staged transition from
visible reasoning to latent work,but its reported setup uses pretrained models
and procedural supervision. It does not demonstrate this natural-source-only
from-scratch proposal. [The Pitfalls of Next-Token Prediction](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.06963)
identifies teacher-forcing shortcuts on constructed planning tasks;it does not
establish that ordinary language pretraining generally fails.

No curriculum is admitted by the audit. A later protocol must freeze the data
view,curriculum,normalization and equally trained one-pass/recurrent controls,
plus a separately cooled work-matched one-pass challenger. Measure generated
conclusions as well as target loss,and retain broad-text NLL separately.
Existing checkpoint screening cannot establish a from-scratch result. Evaluation
must exclude source groups seen during their original pretraining,including
full worked solutions. Fresh confirmation and the charter's five-seed/error-
budget rules remain intact. No architecture,RL,teacher or router is added.
