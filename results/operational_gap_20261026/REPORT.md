# Post-stop instance accounting

The baseline stage stopped around07:15 UTC. At the13:21 inspection,its processes
were absent and no managed accelerator workers were running. The interval is
retained as instance opportunity cost,not omitted from research accounting.
A second interval covers the CPU audit reruns and necessary inode remediation
before the new native recovery admission. No accelerator work is assigned in
either gap. The adjacent stage costs are separate and nonoverlapping.
